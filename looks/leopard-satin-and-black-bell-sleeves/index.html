Styling notes

Leopard is autumn's favourite neutral, and a fluid satin midi makes it feel elegant rather than loud. Keep everything else black, a fitted bell-sleeve top, bow Mary Jane heels and a quilted chain bag, then let gold do the talking with chunky dome earrings and a vintage square watch. Cat-eye sunglasses add a little retro attitude for lunches, shopping or date night.
