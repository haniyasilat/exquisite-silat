FAQ

Autumn outfit questions

What's a good autumn outfit idea?

Knitwear, plaid, and warm neutrals — layer a cardigan or bomber jacket over a simple top, and bring in boots and a structured bag.

How do I layer for autumn without overheating?

Choose one heavier piece — a jacket or a chunky cardigan — and keep everything underneath lightweight, so you can remove a layer indoors.
