FAQ

Summer outfit questions

What's a good summer outfit for hot weather?

Breathable fabrics like linen or satin, a maxi skirt or wide-leg jeans, and sandals — keep layers minimal and let one accessory, like a tote or sunglasses, do the styling.

What shoes work best for summer outfits?

Sandals, espadrilles, or kitten heels — anything open and breathable that still pairs with a dressier top if the day calls for it.
