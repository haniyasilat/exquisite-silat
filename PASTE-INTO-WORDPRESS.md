# PASTE INTO WORDPRESS — remodotvision.wordpress.com/wp-admin/

Do these in order. You're already logged in — just copy/paste.

---

## STEP 1 — Make it a blog (30 sec)

1. Left menu → **Settings** → **Reading**
2. Select **"Your latest posts"**
3. Click **Save**

4. Left menu → **Settings** → **General**
5. Site title: `Remodot Vision`
6. Tagline: `Honest guides & product picks`
7. **Save**

---

## STEP 2 — Fix theme (1 min)

1. **Appearance** → **Themes**
2. Search **Retrospect** or **Twenty Twenty-Four** → **Activate**

---

## STEP 3 — Delete old homepage (30 sec)

1. **Pages** → find the RemoVision / computer vision page
2. **Trash** it

---

## STEP 4 — Add About page

**Pages → Add New** → Title: `About`

Paste this:

```
Welcome to Remodot Vision — honest guides and product picks for everyday wellness.

We share practical tips and recommendations. Some links are affiliate links — we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Thank you for reading.
```

→ **Publish**

**Pages → Add New** → Title: `Affiliate Disclosure`

**What it does**
Rosemary essential oil is often used in scalp massage routines. Many people use it consistently for 3–6 months as part of a natural hair care routine.

**How to use it**
1. Dilute 3–5 drops in a tablespoon of carrier oil (coconut or jojoba)
2. Massage into scalp 2–3 times per week
3. Leave on 30+ minutes, then shampoo
